Features

What's in the cartridge.

Anatomical landmarks

A one-piece airway with palpable thyroid cartilage, cricoid cartilage and tracheal rings, with separate cricothyrotomy and tracheostomy regions.

Prototype

Landmarks by touch

Nothing is printed or marked on the skin. Learners palpate to find the structures they need, as they would on a patient.

Layered materials

Silicone skin over a softer silicone subcutaneous layer, cast around a flexible 3D-printed airway, with a simulated cricothyroid membrane.

Prototype materials

Two skills, one cartridge

The cricothyrotomy site and a separate, cuttable tracheal ring region stay distinct, so both procedures can be practiced on the same cartridge.

Low maintenance

All cutting happens in the cartridge, and the base stays clean. Cleaning and care instructions to be confirmed.

Simulated bleeding

A sealed fluid strip under the skin for passive bleeding on incision, with no external reservoirs.

In development

Cric/Trach applications

One cartridge, two airway skills.

The combined cartridge supports simulation practice for both procedures. CT1 is a training aid and works alongside your program's own curriculum and protocols.

01 · Cricothyrotomy

Emergency front-of-neck access, rehearsed.

Designed for repeated cricothyrotomy practice in a controlled, simulated setting, so a rarely performed skill can be rehearsed on a regular schedule.

  • Identifying the cricothyroid membrane by palpation
  • Incision through layered skin and subcutaneous tissue
  • Tactile feedback on entry through the simulated membrane Prototype
  • Bougie and tube passage into an open airway lumen
02 · Tracheostomy

Below the cricoid, on the same cartridge.

A dedicated tracheal ring region keeps tracheostomy practice separate from the cricothyrotomy site.

  • Locating tracheal rings by touch
  • Practice on a dedicated, cuttable ring region
  • The same cartridge design for every learner
  • A fresh cartridge for each learner after cutting
