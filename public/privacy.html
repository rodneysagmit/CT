Privacy notice

What we collect

When you contact us through the inquiry form or by email, we receive what you choose to send: your name, organization, job title and organization type, email address, phone number, your message, any quote details (such as quantities, timeline, payment method, ship-to location and tax-exempt status) and how you heard about us.

How we use it

We use your information only to respond to your inquiry: to answer questions, arrange demonstrations, prepare quotes and follow up about ClinTrainers products. We do not sell or rent your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

How it reaches us

Inquiry form submissions are delivered to our inbox by a third-party form service. If you email us directly, your message is handled through our email provider. These providers process your information only to deliver it to us.

Cookies and tracking

This site does not use advertising or tracking cookies. Your browser may remember your Cards or List choice on the cartridges page; that preference stays on your device. Like most websites, our hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, for security and reliability.

How long we keep it

We keep inquiry information for as long as we need it to respond to you and maintain our business relationship. You can ask us to update or delete your information at any time.
