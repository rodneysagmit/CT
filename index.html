CT1 product site: four layout options.

One single-page site for the CT1 cricothyrotomy & tracheostomy task trainer, built four ways. Every option uses the same copy, specs, CAD renders, live 3D model, colors and type. Only the layout and motion change. Open each one on a computer and on a phone, then pick a direction.

Each page has a bar at the bottom for moving between options and getting back here.

Headline and CTAs on the left, 3D model on the right. The most familiar product-page pattern.

Review notes

Shared by all four

  • Same content: hero, overview, features, cricothyrotomy and tracheostomy applications, gallery, specs, what's included, FAQ, demo CTA.
  • Live 3D model from the CAD files, with a still image while it loads. Drag or use arrow keys; Pause and Reset are always visible.
  • "Request a Demo" opens a pre-filled email to inquiry@clintrainers.com.
  • No pricing, dates, certifications, testimonials or outcome claims. Unconfirmed details appear as dashed italic placeholders.
  • Reduced motion turns off auto-rotation, reveals and background animation.

What to look at

  • Which hero gives the best first impression of the trainer?
  • Which layout makes the features and specs easiest to scan?
  • How does each feel on a phone?
  • Is the motion restrained enough?
  • Any copy or spec corrections. They apply to every option.
