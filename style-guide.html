3D viewer

The hero centerpiece.

Model
Real CAD assembly: base, rubber pad, cartridge, cover, airway insert, nameplate. The Base Lid is not shown. Desktop file about 640 KB; phones get a 420 KB version.
Look
Soft studio lighting, teal and ice rim lights, soft floor shadow, transparent over the hero glow.
Motion
Slow auto-rotation. On first load the cartridge settles into the base.
Controls
Drag or arrow keys (Shift for bigger steps), Pause/Play, Reset. No zoom, so the page still scrolls.
Framing
Same camera as the still image, so the swap is seamless. Each option sets distance and offset in its HTML (data-cam-dist, data-view-shift).
Fallbacks
Still image without 3D support. A labeled placeholder box if no model is set. No auto-rotation with reduced motion.
Swapping
Export GLB, compress with gltfpack, set the path in assets/js/config.js.

Motion

Subtle on purpose.

What moves

  • 3D model: slow auto-rotation and a one-time cartridge drop-in (1.6 s).
  • Hero background: faint grid and slow airflow lines.
  • Scroll: sections fade up 18 px as they enter; smooth scroll from links; header turns to dark glass.
  • Hover: teal halo on buttons; card borders lift; images scale 3–4%.
  • Option 3: chapter image crossfades as each chapter reaches mid-screen.

Guardrails

  • Easing: cubic-bezier(.2, .7, .2, 1); reveals 0.9 s.
  • The 3D loads after the page and stops when off screen or in a hidden tab.
  • Phones get a lighter model, lower resolution and smaller shadows.
  • Reduce motion: no auto-rotation, intro, reveals or airflow lines. Pause shows as already on.

Content rules

Training equipment, stated plainly.

Voice

  • Clear, clinical, confident. Short sentences.
  • Present CT1 as simulation training equipment for education only.
  • Specs and features come from the development record and the CAD. Prototype details are labeled.
  • Unconfirmed details stay visible as TBD until verified.

Never on the site

  • Pricing or availability dates (handled during the inquiry).
  • Certifications, endorsements, testimonials or outcome claims.
  • Step-by-step procedure instructions.
  • A stand-in model presented as the real product.
